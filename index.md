# Reverie Practice Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 July 2026

**Contact:** reverieapp@outlook.com

Reverie Practice ("Reverie", "we", "our", or "the app") is a macOS therapy practice management app designed for local-first, private record keeping. This policy explains what information the app stores, what data may leave the device when optional features are used, and how that data is handled.

This policy covers Reverie Practice as an app and service. Therapists and practices using Reverie remain responsible for their own client privacy notices, professional obligations, consent processes, records management, and legal compliance.

## Summary

- Reverie stores core practice data locally on your Mac by default.
- Sensitive app records are protected with encryption.
- Reverie does not sell personal data.
- Reverie does not use advertising trackers.
- Reverie does not include third-party analytics SDKs.
- Optional features may contact Apple or third-party services only when needed for app functionality, such as Calendar Sync, CPD/CE event discovery and region-based filtering, resource viewing, podcast or video metadata lookup, crash diagnostics, or support.
- Address and postcode lookup is unavailable in this build. Addresses entered manually remain in Reverie's local records and are not sent to a lookup provider.

## Information Stored Locally

Reverie may store the following information locally on the user's Mac, depending on how the app is used:

- Practice details, such as practice name, addresses, contact details, invoicing preferences, and payment wording.
- Client, group, couple, family, guardian, and key contact records.
- Session records, session notes, process notes, tasks, actions, holidays, endings, and attendance information.
- Invoice records, invoice PDFs, CPD/CE entries, supervision records, journal entries, email templates, resource records, annotations, bookmarks, and imported files.
- App settings, security settings, encrypted backups, and local diagnostic logs.

This local app data is not automatically sent to Reverie. Users control exports, backups, email drafts, and support sharing.

## Data That May Leave the Device

The following data may be transmitted off the device when specific optional features are used.

| Data type | Why it may be used | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| System locale or manual region | Reverie may use the Mac's system locale or a user-selected manual region to filter CPD/CE events and locale-specific content. Reverie does not request device location for this. | App functionality |
| Search history | Search terms or lookup queries may be sent when using features such as podcast search, RSS/resource lookup, YouTube/SoundCloud metadata lookup, or CPD/CE event discovery. | App functionality |
| Browsing history | Website or resource URLs may be loaded in the app's web/resource viewer or opened externally when the user chooses to view web content. | App functionality |
| Calendar event data | If Calendar Sync is enabled, Reverie stores a one-way mirror in the selected writable calendar. Therapy-session titles can use Session code, First name, Name + code, or Busy only. Supervision entries can include the supervisor, peer, or group name, and explicitly bookmarked Events use their event title. Mirrored data is limited to the title, date and time, timezone, and busy status. Reverie does not add notes, contact details, clinical content, exact locations, alarms, or completed CPD records. | App functionality |
| Crash data | Apple may provide crash reports and diagnostic information to developers for users who have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers. | App functionality |

Reverie does not use these data types for tracking, advertising, or marketing.

Calendar Sync is optional and requires full Calendar access so Reverie can find, update, reconcile, and remove the events it previously created. Reverie restricts its work to the calendar selected by the user and identifies its own events with opaque markers. Calendar copies are outside Reverie's encrypted database and are governed by the user's selected calendar provider. Users can pause syncing or disconnect while choosing whether existing mirrored events should be kept or removed.

## iCloud Backups

App-managed iCloud backup is unavailable in this build. Reverie does not upload app-managed backup files or clinical databases to iCloud in this build.

Users can still create local encrypted backups and choose where to store them. Users remain responsible for how they store or copy those local backup files outside Reverie.

## Email and Support

Reverie may help create email drafts through the user's chosen email app. When the user asks Reverie to draft an email, the app may use Apple Events to open Apple Mail or Microsoft Outlook, insert the draft message, and attach generated invoice PDFs or user-selected files. Reverie does not send emails automatically. Email content is sent only when the user chooses to send it through their email provider.

If a user contacts support, we use the information provided only to investigate and respond to the request. Users should avoid sending client-identifiable information unless it is necessary and they have the appropriate authority to do so. Debug logs are intended to redact sensitive information, but users should review any support material before sending it.

## Third-Party Services

Reverie may interact with third-party services to provide app functionality, including but not limited to:

- Apple services, including EventKit Calendar, Speech, Translation, and crash diagnostics.
- Podcast, RSS, video, audio, resource, and event-discovery providers.
- The user's selected calendar provider when Calendar Sync is explicitly enabled.
- The user's chosen email provider or email client.

These services may process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms. Reverie uses third-party services only for the app functionality described in this policy and expects those services to protect received data according to their published privacy and security commitments. Reverie does not use third-party services to track users across apps or websites.

CPD/CE event discovery uses public listing metadata where enabled, keeps source attribution links, and opens external booking/provider pages only when the user chooses to view or book an event.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Reverie does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or data brokers. Reverie does not track users across apps or websites.

## Security

Reverie is designed around local-first storage, encryption, privacy-preserving logging, and user-controlled exports and backups. Users remain responsible for protecting their Mac account, app password, backup passwords, exported files, and any files or emails they choose to share outside the app.

## Data Export and Deletion

Users can export app data using the export and backup tools provided in Reverie. Users can also delete records inside the app or use available reset/delete workflows where provided. Optional features can be stopped by disabling the feature in Reverie, revoking macOS permissions in System Settings, or stopping use of the relevant calendar, email, backup, or support-sharing workflow.

Because Reverie stores core records locally, deletion of local records is controlled from the user's Mac. Data held by Apple, calendar providers, email providers, or other third-party services is governed by those services' own deletion and retention processes.

## Children's Data

Reverie is intended for use by therapists and practice administrators, not by children as direct app users. The app may store records relating to child or young-person clients when entered by a therapist or practice. The therapist or practice is responsible for obtaining appropriate consent and meeting applicable legal and professional duties.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Reverie's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version is published as a Markdown document in Reverie's public GitHub repository at the privacy policy URL supplied in App Store Connect.
