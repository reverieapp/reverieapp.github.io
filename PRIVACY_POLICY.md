# Reverie Practice Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 May 2026  
**Contact:** reverieapp@outlook.com

Reverie Practice ("Reverie", "we", "our", or "the app") is a macOS therapy practice management app designed for local-first, private record keeping. This policy explains what information the app stores, what data may leave the device when optional features are used, and how that data is handled.

This policy covers Reverie Practice as an app and service. Therapists and practices using Reverie remain responsible for their own client privacy notices, professional obligations, consent processes, records management, and legal compliance.

## Summary

- Reverie stores core practice data locally on your Mac by default.
- Sensitive app records are protected with encryption.
- Reverie does not sell personal data.
- Reverie does not use advertising trackers.
- Reverie does not include third-party analytics SDKs.
- Optional features may contact Apple or third-party services only when needed for app functionality, such as address lookup, region-based event filtering, resource viewing, podcast or video metadata lookup, iCloud backup, crash diagnostics, or support.

## Information Stored Locally

Reverie may store the following information locally on the user's Mac, depending on how the app is used:

- Practice details, such as practice name, addresses, contact details, invoicing preferences, and payment wording.
- Client, group, couple, family, guardian, and key contact records.
- Session records, session notes, process notes, tasks, actions, holidays, endings, and attendance information.
- Invoice records, invoice PDFs, CPD entries, supervision records, journal entries, email templates, resource records, annotations, bookmarks, and imported files.
- App settings, security settings, encrypted backups, and local diagnostic logs.

This local app data is not automatically sent to Reverie. Users control exports, backups, email drafts, and support sharing.

## Data That May Leave the Device

The following data may be transmitted off the device when specific optional features are used.

| Data type | Why it may be used | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Physical address | Address, postcode, or place lookup may send entered address text to Apple Maps, Google Maps, Geoapify, Postcodes.io, Zippopotam.us, Ordnance Survey, GetAddress.io, Nominatim/OpenStreetMap, PlaceKit, or similar lookup providers, depending on app configuration and availability. | App functionality |
| Coarse location | The app may request region or country-level location to filter CPD events and locale-specific content. If location permission is not granted, Reverie falls back to the device locale. | App functionality |
| Search history | Search terms or lookup queries may be sent when using features such as address lookup, podcast search, RSS/resource lookup, YouTube/SoundCloud metadata lookup, or CPD/event discovery. | App functionality |
| Browsing history | Website or resource URLs may be loaded in the app's web/resource viewer or opened externally when the user chooses to view web content. | App functionality |
| Crash data | Apple may provide crash reports and diagnostic information to developers for users who have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers. | App functionality |

Reverie does not use these data types for tracking, advertising, or marketing.

## iCloud Backups

If the user enables iCloud backup, Reverie uploads encrypted backup files to the user's Apple iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. Backup files may include app databases and backup-supported files or resources, depending on the backup type selected.

Reverie does not use iCloud backup for advertising or tracking. Users can enable, disable, create, restore, and delete backups from within the app.

## Email and Support

Reverie may help create email drafts through the user's chosen email app. Email content is sent only when the user chooses to send it through their email provider.

If a user contacts support, we use the information provided only to investigate and respond to the request. Users should avoid sending client-identifiable information unless it is necessary and they have the appropriate authority to do so. Debug logs are intended to redact sensitive information, but users should review any support material before sending it.

## Third-Party Services

Reverie may interact with third-party services to provide app functionality, including but not limited to:

- Apple services, including iCloud, CloudKit, MapKit, Speech, Translation, and crash diagnostics.
- Address and place lookup providers.
- Podcast, RSS, video, audio, resource, and event-discovery providers.
- The user's chosen email provider or email client.

These services may process requests according to their own privacy policies. Reverie does not use third-party services to track users across apps or websites.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Reverie does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or data brokers. Reverie does not track users across apps or websites.

## Security

Reverie is designed around local-first storage, encryption, privacy-preserving logging, and user-controlled exports and backups. Users remain responsible for protecting their Mac account, app password, backup passwords, exported files, and any files or emails they choose to share outside the app.

## Data Export and Deletion

Users can export app data using the export and backup tools provided in Reverie. Users can also delete records inside the app or use available reset/delete workflows where provided.

Because Reverie stores core records locally, deletion of local records is controlled from the user's Mac. Data held by Apple, email providers, address lookup providers, or other third-party services is governed by those services' own deletion and retention processes.

## Children's Data

Reverie is intended for use by therapists and practice administrators, not by children as direct app users. The app may store records relating to child or young-person clients when entered by a therapist or practice. The therapist or practice is responsible for obtaining appropriate consent and meeting applicable legal and professional duties.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Reverie's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published at the privacy policy URL supplied in App Store Connect.
